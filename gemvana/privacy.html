Privacy Policy

Gemvana: Block Puzzle · Effective date: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how Vlad Matei Catana Bota ("we", "us"), the developer of the mobile game Gemvana: Block Puzzle (the "App"), handles information. Contact: contact@varful.ro, 540400 Târgu Mureș, Romania.

1. Summary

  • You can play without an account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • Your game progress (coins, scores, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
  • Signing in to Game Center or Google Play Games is optional; it is used only for achievements and leaderboards.

3. Advertising and consent

The App uses Google AdMob. Google may use cookies and device identifiers to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the App we show Google's consent form (IAB TCF). You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options.
  • iOS: we ask for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change it in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • If you buy "Remove Ads", banners and interstitial ads are no longer shown. Optional rewarded videos remain available when you choose to watch them.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Consent (personalized advertising, storing/accessing information on your device), performance of a contract (in-app purchases), and legitimate interests (non-personalized ads that keep the game free, security and fraud prevention).

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, e.g. 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

6. Retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Data processed by Google is retained according to Google's Privacy Policy. Because we do not keep accounts or personal data on our servers, uninstalling the App removes the data we control.

7. International transfers

Google and its partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Romania: ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro). Contact us at contact@varful.ro.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
